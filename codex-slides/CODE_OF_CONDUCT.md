# Code of Conduct

Codex Slides is an open, collaborative project. Contributors and maintainers
are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and confidential materials.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Using project spaces to promote illegal or abusive activity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate these expectations. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
